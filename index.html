About Us

WAFU Brothers was founded in 2009,
one of the earliest manufacturers in China to enter the leak testing instrument industry.

WAFU Brothers (Shenzhen) Automation Technology Co., Ltd. is a high-tech manufacturer specializing in the research, development, production, and sales of various types of leak testers, sealing integrity testing systems, IPX waterproof testing equipment, and customized fixtures and tooling. Its products are widely used in industries such as automotive, medical devices, aerospace, defense, consumer electronics, security systems, and home appliances.

With 17 years of industry experience, WAFU Brothers has provided products and services to over 8,000 customers worldwide, including well-known domestic and international enterprises such as Audi, BYD, Xiaomi, and General Motors. The company’s market presence spans across the globe.

The company is supported by a professional R&D team, a dedicated pre-sales and after-sales service team, and modern manufacturing facilities. From product design and quality to customer experience, as well as software and hardware development, every detail reflects the team’s commitment and focus. WAFU Brothers consistently upholds the principle that “providing optimal leak testing solutions and satisfactory service to customers” is its top priority.
