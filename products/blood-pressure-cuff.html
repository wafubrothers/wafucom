Product Introduction

  • 10-Channel Customized Blood Pressure Cuff Leak Testing Cabinet is a high-precision leak testing equipment specially developed for the medical device industry, designed for sealing performance testing of blood pressure cuff products.
  • This equipment adopts an integrated structure and supports fully customized solutions — the number of testing channels, testing process, and cabinet dimensions can be customized according to production capacity, interface size, test pressure, and production line layout.
  • This machine is one of the few 10-channel leak testing systems available, making it suitable for high-volume blood pressure cuff manufacturers. Based on high-sensitivity differential pressure/direct pressure leak testing technology, it achieves testing accuracy up to ±0.1% F.S., quickly identifying micro-level leaks and effectively detecting sealing defects in blood pressure cuffs.

Leak Testing Technologies & Methods

Testing Components

Includes an air source treatment unit, pressure regulation module, and precision sensors to form a complete leak testing system.

Internal Pressure Testing Method

Gas is introduced into the test part at a specified pressure, and pressure changes are monitored to evaluate sealing performance. Suitable for enclosed cavity testing.

Direct Pressure (Pressure Decay) Testing Method

Test pressure is applied directly and pressure decay is monitored, making it ideal for production environments requiring high testing efficiency.

Core Advantages

High-Precision Testing

Equipped with imported high-precision pressure sensors (accuracy ±0.1% F.S.) and a 24-bit AD acquisition chip. Detection sensitivity reaches 0.1 Pa, ensuring accurate and reliable test results.

High-Speed Testing

Optimized inflation and testing process, combined with a fast-response sensor system. The testing cycle for a single product can be reduced to less than 1 second, greatly boosting production efficiency.

Flexible Testing Modes

Supports both positive and negative pressure testing methods. Built-in multiple test programs allow custom parameter settings according to product characteristics, meeting diverse application needs.
