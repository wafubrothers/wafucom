Latest Premium Leak Tester

Industry-leading precision. The tenth-generation premium leak tester uses 24-bit A/D conversion, with sensitivity down to 0.02 Pa, 0.1 Pa measurement accuracy, and repeatability within 1 Pa. It can identify micron-scale leak paths for demanding applications from consumer electronics to new-energy vehicle components.

Temperature compensation and advanced filtering suppress workshop temperature drift, air-source pulsation, and electromagnetic noise. The result is stable pass/fail judgment near presses and welders and across large day-to-night temperature changes, reducing false rejects and unnecessary rework.

Direct-pressure, differential-pressure, flow, and vacuum methods can be configured for different products and standards. Positive-pressure testing suits products that must remain dry, while vacuum testing supports faster production cycles.

0.1 Pa accuracy for precision seal and leak-rate testing Multiple test modes with flexible product-specific parameters Multichannel expansion for high-volume production Stable, traceable results for production quality management

Custom Leak Testing Cabinet

This highly integrated custom system combines the leak tester, dedicated fixture, and control system in one compact platform. Noise suppression and temperature compensation deliver 0.1 Pa resolution, repeatability within 1 Pa, and cycle times as fast as three seconds.

The process and fixture can be tailored to product dimensions, seal locations, process requirements, and line takt time. Automatic clamping, recipe control, pass/fail judgment, and data recording support standardized, traceable quality control.

The cabinet can integrate loading, testing, sorting, and data upload with automated lines. Its precision, speed, and customization suit automotive components, medical devices, consumer electronics, home appliances, aerospace, and other demanding high-volume applications.

0.01 Pa sensitivity for minute leak detection Integrated tester, fixture, and control system Automatic clamping, judgment, and data recording Custom line integration matched to product and takt time

Water Immersion Leak Point Detector

This high-precision immersion tester checks sealing and pinpoints the exact leak location. It pressurizes the part and submerges it so escaping bubbles reveal minute defects quickly and directly.

The fixture seals and fills the workpiece before lowering it smoothly into the tank. Pressure, hold time, and sequence can be adjusted for different structures so operators obtain clear, reliable results.

Unlike pass/fail-only methods, bubble testing identifies whether the defect is at a seal, weld, housing, or connector. It supports sample validation, production sampling, and failed-part diagnosis and can complement an automated leak tester.

Adjustable 0-90 kPa test range Simulates water depths up to 300 meters Visible bubbles identify the exact leak location Ideal for diagnosing castings and electronic housings
